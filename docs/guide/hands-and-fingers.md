# Hands and fingers


With **Fix Hands** on (the default, full version), QuickMotion lines up your character's wrists and fingers with
GASP's own mannequin when it builds the character. Without it, characters whose hands rest differently from GASP's
mannequin keep that difference in every move. It's typically 15 to 25 degrees: hands that stay half-closed or
hover instead of opening and landing on a ledge the way GASP's mannequin does.

QuickMotion measures this against GASP's mannequin while it walks, runs and vaults. With Fix Hands on, wrists stay
within 5 degrees and fingers within 8 degrees on average, on MetaHuman, Mixamo, VRoid and custom characters.

Turn **Fix Hands** off only if you want to set up the hands yourself in the IK Retargeter. Then click **Rebuild**.
