# 3. Play



1. Open GASP's level (`Content/Levels/DefaultLevel`) if it isn't open already.
2. Drag **BP_GASP_\<Name\>** from the new folder into the level.
3. With it selected, find **Auto Possess Player** in the **Details** panel and set it to **Player 0**.
4. Press **Play**.

You're now playing as your character with GASP's motion matching: walk, sprint, jump, and vault or mantle onto
GASP's boxes with GASP's usual controls. To make your character the player every time without placing it by hand, see
[Make it your player](../guide/make-it-your-player.md#make-it-your-player).
