# 4. Change it later



Open the settings card (`GASP_<Name>_Setup`) whenever you want to change something:

- **Character Mesh**: pick a different mesh (for example an updated version of your character).
- Click **Rebuild**. QuickMotion refreshes its helper files. Your `BP_GASP_<Name>` and everything you added to it stay
  exactly as they were.
- **Open Character** opens `BP_GASP_<Name>` for you.

That's it: your character runs, jumps and vaults with GASP. The [User Guide](../guide/the-settings-card.md) covers the settings card,
using the character in your own game, and upgrading from Lite.
